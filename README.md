## Featured projects



### 💊 Euler vs RK4 on a Vancomycin PK Model

Undergraduate thesis comparing two numerical methods on a one-compartment drug model.

- **Problem:** Which method stays accurate and stable at practical step sizes?

- **Approach:** Derived the steady-state concentration (about 144.3 mg/L), computed stability bounds, convergence orders and RMSE.

- **Result:** RK4 stays stable up to about 20 h steps, while Euler fails beyond about 14 h.

- **Stack:** `Python` · `LaTeX`
